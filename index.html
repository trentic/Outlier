How the outlier score works

Outlier score = video views ÷ median views of the channel's recent uploads. A score of 5× means the video got five times as many views as a typical video on that channel.

  • The baseline is the channel's last N uploads (Advanced → Baseline size). It leaves out the video being scored and any upload younger than 48 hours, which hasn't collected its views yet.
  • Shorts are compared with the channel's other Shorts, and long-form videos with its other long-form videos. If a channel has fewer than 5 uploads of that format, all of its uploads are used.
  • A video counts as a Short if it runs 3 minutes or less, or has #shorts in its title or description.
  • Hashtag mode searches for #tag. With the strict option on, it keeps only videos whose title, description or tags contain that hashtag. That's the closest the API gets to a YouTube hashtag page.
  • Channel baselines are cached in this browser for 12 hours, so repeat searches cost less quota.
